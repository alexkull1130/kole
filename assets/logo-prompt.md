Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: logo mark for the Kole programming language. Create one intentionally poorly drawn lump of coal, as if a child quickly drew it with black and dark gray wax crayons. A squat irregular angular rock silhouette, clumsy wobbly thick outlines, uneven scribbled crayon fill, a few awkward gray facet lines, visible waxy grain and little gaps in the strokes. Charming amateur doodle, simple and recognizable even small, decidedly not polished vector art or realistic rock rendering. Single centered coal lump occupying about 70 percent of a square canvas with generous clear margins. Genuinely transparent background, preserve crayon texture in the coal itself. No text, no letters, no face, no eyes, no limbs, no extra objects, no shadow, no border, no watermark.
